\section*{Profile}
I am a mathematician based in Denver, Colorado, and a Visiting Professor at Sam Houston State University, effective September 1, 2026. My research is in logic and set theory. I previously served as a tenured Associate Professor of Mathematics at Sam Houston State University until August 31, 2026. My work combines research in Ramsey theory, ultrafilters, and foundations of mathematics with sustained teaching innovation in undergraduate and graduate mathematics. My teaching is grounded in backward design, constructive alignment, active learning, mastery-oriented assessment, public mathematical writing, and mentorship through independent study and student research.\par
